# Data Contract and Quality

## Purpose
Determine whether data is structurally valid, fit for use and handled appropriately when controls fail.

## Invoke when
- adding a validation rule;
- changing quarantine behaviour;
- defining trusted-data criteria.

## Review questions
- Is this a structural failure, invalid record or valid audit exception?
- What risk does the control address?
- What happens when it fails?
- Does the control improve fitness for use?

## Evidence expected
- control identifier and reason;
- deterministic failure behaviour;
- tests covering the control;
- reconciliation after quarantine.

## Failure signal
A control exists without a clear risk, response or fitness-for-use consequence.
