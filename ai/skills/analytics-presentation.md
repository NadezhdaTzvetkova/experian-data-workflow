# Analytics and Presentation

## Purpose
Review analytical correctness, stakeholder usefulness and presentation clarity.

## Invoke when
- defining KPIs;
- adding analytical outputs;
- creating stakeholder visuals or narrative.

## Review questions
- Is each metric calculated from trusted data?
- Are overlapping flags interpreted correctly?
- Does each visual answer a useful question?
- Are limitations visible?

## Evidence expected
- curated analytical source;
- independently verified headline KPIs;
- concise stakeholder observations;
- explicit interpretation limits.

## Failure signal
A chart is visually convincing but its analytical meaning or provenance is unclear.
